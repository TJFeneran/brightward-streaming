# Layout patterns adapted from the Elastic reference

Reference: https://elastic.github.io/observability-team/collaterals/observability-presentation/company-preso.html

The linked presentation's cover uses a large left headline and a right-side
isometric platform, short external leaders, generous margins and a quiet footer.
Use that balance as a reference, with Brightward colors and subject matter.

- Cover: one plain claim on the left, one explanatory diagram on the right.
- Process: three to five labeled stages with thin connectors and a human review
  endpoint. A future action branch needs a visible future label.
- Evidence: one sourced observation or actual app screenshot with a short claim.
  Keep the source and synthetic-data disclosure readable.
- Outcome: one honest metric or target with its qualification, paired with a
  minimal comparison or a concrete next step.

These are reusable composition patterns, not an authored deck or a requirement
to add slides. There is no copied Elastic product geometry or screenshot asset.
